# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Owners, managers, accountants, and sales staff working inside an Arabic RTL car
showroom. They use the system throughout the workday to manage inventory,
customers, sales, purchases, installments, accounting, and reports.

## Product Purpose

Provide one dependable operational system for running a multi-branch car
showroom. Success means users can complete daily work quickly while financial
records, permissions, and branch boundaries remain clear and trustworthy.

## Brand Personality

Automotive, confident, precise.

## Anti-references

- Generic blue SaaS dashboards with interchangeable cards.
- Decorative automotive landing pages that hide operational information.
- Excessive glass effects, neon gradients, and continuous distracting motion.
- Any design that weakens Arabic RTL readability or accounting clarity.

## Design Principles

1. Make the showroom identity visible without compromising the work surface.
2. Use cinematic automotive moments for orientation, then return to precise UI.
3. Keep financial and operational states explicit and readable.
4. Make interactions feel tactile, fast, and consistent.
5. Preserve familiar workflows and reveal complexity progressively.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, complete keyboard focus states, responsive layouts, and
full support for `prefers-reduced-motion`. Status must never depend on color
alone.
